# My feed preferences

This is the rubric the AI uses to decide what reaches my feed. Edit it freely —
plain language is fine. The more specific the examples, the better it gets.

## What I WANT to see

- **Architecture & design** — buildings, Icelandic building design, architects and
  firms, design news. A story about a *person* only if they're an architect,
  designer, or architecture student. (byggingar.is and mbl Heimili are all on-topic.)
- **Real estate** — notable or unusual properties for sale, the housing market,
  prices, supply.
- **Planning & city construction (borgarskipulag)** — deiliskipulag, byggingarleyfi,
  municipal and government building projects, infrastructure (bridges, tunnels, roads).
- **Economy & policy that actually bites me** — taxes, **inflation (verðbólga)**,
  interest rates (vextir, stýrivextir), kílómetragjald, housing policy, EU/ESB
  accession talks and the referendum, major legislation. I especially want inflation
  news. Substance, not party PR.
- **Hard domestic news** — murder, serious crime, prison incidents, major
  accidents or disasters *in Iceland*. Foreign crime/disaster only if it's
  genuinely world-historic.
- **Culture with an Iceland hook** — a film being shot here, concerts here, a
  famous person visiting, a concert or festival that turned into a fiasco,
  nightlife news (a bar shut down overnight), Icelandic film industry news.
- **Genuinely huge global entertainment** — a major rapper or actor death,
  scandal, or legal blow-up. High bar: a death or a crime, not a feelings interview.

When unsure but it's inside one of my topics above, KEEP it. Lean toward keeping.

## What I do NOT want

- Sport and football.
- Routine celebrity and gossip, emotional interviews.
- Human-interest fluff — "a random Icelander does a thing" with no design angle.
- Lifestyle / health / sex clickbait.
- Freak-weather novelty.

## Language note (important)

Articles are Icelandic. Read idiom in context, never literally. "skaut á" means
to take verbal jabs at someone, NOT to shoot — so "Martin skaut á foreldra sína"
is personal drama, a DISCARD.
