# My feed preferences

This is the rubric the AI uses to decide what reaches my feed. Edit it freely —
plain language is fine. The more specific the examples, the better it gets.

## What I WANT to see

- **Architecture & design** — buildings, Icelandic building design, architects and
  firms, design news. A story about a *person* only if they're an architect,
  designer, or architecture student.
- **Real estate** — notable or unusual properties for sale, the housing market,
  prices, supply. (Example I'd keep: "Hildur selur einstakt einbýli í Garðabæ".)
- **Planning & city construction** — deiliskipulag, byggingarleyfi, municipal and
  government building projects, infrastructure (bridges, tunnels, roads).
  (Example I'd keep: "Synjaði umdeildu deiliskipulagi á Suðurlandsbraut".)
- **Economy & policy that actually bites me** — taxes, inflation, interest rates,
  kílómetragjald, housing policy, EU/ESB accession talks and the referendum,
  major legislation. Substance, not party PR.
- **Hard domestic news** — murder, serious crime, prison incidents, major
  accidents or disasters *in Iceland*. Foreign crime/disaster only if it's
  genuinely world-historic.
- **Culture with an Iceland hook** — a film being shot here, concerts here, a
  famous person visiting, a concert or festival that turned into a fiasco,
  nightlife news (a bar shut down overnight, scene happenings), Icelandic film
  industry news.
- **Genuinely huge global entertainment** — a major rapper or actor death,
  scandal, or legal blow-up. High bar: a death or a crime, not a feelings
  interview.

When unsure but it's inside one of my topics above, KEEP it. Lean toward keeping.

## What I do NOT want

- Sport and football.
- Routine celebrity and gossip, emotional interviews. (Example I'd drop: "Madonna
  opnar sig um erfitt samband við dóttur".)
- Human-interest fluff — "a random Icelander does a thing" with no design angle.
- Lifestyle / health / sex clickbait. (Example I'd drop: "Þetta gerir kynlífið
  miklu betra".)
- Freak-weather novelty. (Example I'd drop: "Rúður sprungu í 400 gráðu hita" — it
  has building-ish words but it's a heatwave novelty, not building news.)

## Language note (important)

The articles are Icelandic. Read idiom in context, do not translate literally.
For example "skaut á" means *to take verbal jabs at* someone, NOT to shoot them —
so "Martin skaut á foreldra sína" is personal drama, a DISCARD, not a crime story.
